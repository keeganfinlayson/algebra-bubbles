    Algebra Bubbles

    1. Choose a practice type and click New expression. Make your own opens the entry bar and keypad.
    2. Drag boxes to reorder them, drop one onto a like term to combine, or drag upward to make a bubble. Bubble lifts the whole expression.
    3. Click matching bubbles in any order. While dragging a bubble, move the pointer over other matching bubbles to select them too, then release to combine. Unlike terms never combine. Click empty space or press Escape to clear all selections.
    4. Explore combines automatically. Solve asks for the resulting term beside the visible expression. After two incorrect attempts the answer is shown and the combination continues.
    5. Drag a bubble to the board or click Unbubble to return all. Select a history step to highlight its current result; Undo restores the previous combination.

    Supported: constants, x, y, x², y², xy. You can type x^2, y^2 and x*y. Use Alt + arrow keys to reorder board terms. Drag the divider or use its arrow keys to resize the sidebar. Neutral colors removes color hints.
